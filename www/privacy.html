Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026 · Last updated 26 July 2026

This policy describes how the social-media publishing service operated at postiz.dailylatin.org (the “Service”) handles data. The Service is a self-hosted installation of Postiz used to compose, schedule, and publish posts to third-party social-media accounts on behalf of their owners. It is operated by an individual, reachable at joe.ndrsn@gmail.com (the “Operator”).

Scope

This policy covers only the operation of the publishing tool itself. It is not a policy about medical, clinical, or patient information: the Service is used solely to publish marketing and informational content that the Operator authors, and it does not collect, store, or process patient or health data.

What data the Service holds

  • Social account access tokens. When a social-media account (for example, a LinkedIn profile or page) is connected, the provider issues an OAuth access token that authorises the Service to publish on that account’s behalf. These tokens are stored on the Operator’s own server to make scheduled publishing possible.
  • Content you create. The text, images, and scheduling details of posts composed in the Service are stored so they can be published at the chosen time.
  • Basic account profile. The name, email address, and profile identifiers of the connected account, as returned by the social provider, are stored to display which account a post will be sent from.

How the data is used

Data is used exclusively to provide the publishing service — that is, to authenticate with connected social platforms and to publish or schedule the content you create. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising, and it is not used for any purpose unrelated to operating the Service.

Third parties

To publish a post, the Service transmits your content and the relevant access token to the social-media platform you chose (such as LinkedIn), which then handles that content under its own privacy policy. The Service does not otherwise disclose your data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the Operator. Access to the Service is restricted, all traffic is served over HTTPS, and account registration is closed.

Retention and revocation

Access tokens and connected-account data are retained only while the account remains connected. You may disconnect an account at any time from within the Service, or by revoking the Service’s access from your social platform’s settings, at which point the associated token ceases to be usable. On request to the contact address below, the Operator will delete the associated stored data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to joe.ndrsn@gmail.com.
